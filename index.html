About

Saved!
Home — overview stats, progress per group, and anything past due.

Groups — tap a task to view it, then Edit to change details, add a progress note, mark it complete, or reopen it.

Search, Calendar and Settings icons are top right, plus a bell for reminders. Swipe left/right on Home/group screens to switch between them.

Reminders — the bell shows what's due today and overdue. Turn on notifications in Settings to also get an OS-level alert while the app is open. Alerts that arrive even when the app is closed need a bigger backend upgrade, planned for later.

PIN — one shared 6-digit PIN for all devices, asked once per day per device. 3 wrong attempts locks entry for 5 minutes, doubling with each further miss.

Sync dot (top right) — tap it any time to see your last sync status.

Tamil calendar — each day in Calendar shows its Tamil month/date beneath the Gregorian date. Tap a day for full panchangam detail (year name, nakshatram, thithi). Computed astronomically (Lahiri ayanamsa) rather than pulled from a published panchangam, so it may occasionally differ by a day from your local almanac right at a month or thithi boundary.
